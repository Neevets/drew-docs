## 3. Information We Process

We aim to collect and process only information reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Drew.

Depending on how you interact with Drew, we may process the following categories of information.

### 3.1 Discord Information

When Drew is installed on or used within a Discord server, we may process identifiers provided by Discord, including:

- Discord User IDs;
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs;
- Discord Channel IDs;
- Discord Role IDs;
- Discord application and interaction identifiers;
- server configuration and settings;
- moderation and security configuration.

These identifiers are generally used to associate Drew's functionality with the relevant user, server, channel, role, or configuration.

### 3.2 Moderation and Security Data

Where Drew's moderation or security functionality is enabled, we may process information relating to moderation and security events, including:

- the Discord user associated with an action;
- the server where the action occurred;
- the type of action detected or performed;
- relevant Discord IDs;
- timestamps;
- configuration or rule responsible for the action;
- security events and detection information.

This information is used to provide moderation, anti-nuke, security, auditing, and related functionality.

### 3.3 Message Content

Drew may process message content **only where a particular feature requires access to message content**, such as certain moderation or security functionality configured by a server administrator.

Where message content is processed solely to perform such functionality, it is not intentionally retained longer than necessary for that purpose.

Unless a specific feature expressly requires otherwise, **Drew does not store message content as part of its ordinary operation**.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring message-content-dependent functionality appropriately.

### 3.4 Account and License Information

If you create or use a Drew account, we may process information associated with your account and License, including:

- Discord User ID;
- account identifiers;
- License identifiers;
- subscription status;
- activated Discord server IDs;
- License Slot information;
- activation and reassignment timestamps;
- plan and entitlement information.

A Drew License may be associated with up to three Discord servers at the same time, subject to the applicable License terms.

### 3.5 Purchase and Subscription Information

When you purchase a Drew License, payment processing is handled through **Lemon Squeezy**.

We may receive and process information necessary to manage your purchase and License, such as:

- customer identifier;
- email address;
- order identifier;
- subscription identifier;
- License information;
- purchase date;
- subscription status;
- renewal or cancellation status;
- product or plan purchased.

Payment card information is handled by the applicable payment provider and is not intentionally stored by Drew unless expressly stated otherwise.

Lemon Squeezy acts as the merchant of record for purchases made through its platform and processes payment-related personal data under its own terms and privacy policy.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods vary depending on the type and purpose of the data.

For example:

- **License and account data:** retained while the account or License remains active and for a reasonable period afterward where necessary for legitimate operational or legal purposes.
- **Server configuration:** retained while necessary to provide the configured functionality and, where applicable, for a limited period after the License becomes inactive.
- **Moderation and security logs:** retained only for the period necessary for moderation, security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and related legitimate purposes.
- **Technical logs:** retained for a limited period appropriate to security, diagnostics, and reliability.
- **Purchase and billing records:** retained for periods required by applicable accounting, tax, legal, or dispute-resolution requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably possible.
