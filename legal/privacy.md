## 3. Information We Process

We aim to collect and process only information reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Drew.

Depending on how you interact with Drew, we may process the following categories of information.

### 3.1 Discord Information

When Drew is installed on or used within a Discord server, we may process identifiers provided by Discord, including:

- Discord User IDs;
- Discord Server (Guild) IDs;
- Discord Channel IDs;
- Discord Role IDs;
- Discord application and interaction identifiers;
- server configuration and settings;
- moderation and security configuration.

These identifiers are generally used to associate Drew's functionality with the relevant user, server, channel, role, or configuration.

### 3.2 Moderation and Security Data

Where Drew's moderation or security functionality is enabled, we may process information relating to moderation and security events, including:

- the Discord user associated with an action;
- the server where the action occurred;
- the type of action detected or performed;
- relevant Discord IDs;
- timestamps;
- configuration or rule responsible for the action;
- security events and detection information.

This information is used to provide moderation, anti-nuke, security, auditing, and related functionality.

### 3.3 Message Content

Drew may process message content in real time only where required by a moderation, security, or command-related feature enabled or used within a Discord server.

Drew is designed not to retain message content as part of its ordinary database records or operational logs. Message content may be temporarily processed in memory for the time necessary to perform the relevant function, such as evaluating a moderation rule or responding to a command.

We configure our diagnostic and error-monitoring systems to minimize or redact personal data where reasonably possible. However, because diagnostic systems may capture limited technical context when an unexpected error occurs, we cannot guarantee that message content will never be included in an error report or technical record. Where message content is incidentally captured, we will treat it as personal data, restrict access to it, and retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for diagnosis, security, or incident resolution.

Unless a specific feature expressly states otherwise, Drew does not intentionally store message content after the processing necessary to perform the relevant function has been completed.

### 3.4 Account and License Information

If you create or use a Drew account, we may process information associated with your account and License, including:

- Discord User ID;
- account identifiers;
- License identifiers;
- subscription status;
- activated Discord server IDs;
- License Slot information;
- activation and reassignment timestamps;
- plan and entitlement information.

A Drew License may be associated with up to three Discord servers at the same time, subject to the applicable License terms.

### 3.5 Purchase and Subscription Information

When you purchase a Drew License, payment processing is handled through **Lemon Squeezy**.

We may receive and process information necessary to manage your purchase and License, such as:

- customer identifier;
- email address;
- order identifier;
- subscription identifier;
- License information;
- purchase date;
- subscription status;
- renewal or cancellation status;
- product or plan purchased.

Payment card information is handled by the applicable payment provider and is not intentionally stored by Drew unless expressly stated otherwise.

Lemon Squeezy acts as the merchant of record for purchases made through its platform and processes payment-related personal data under its own terms and privacy policy.

### 3.6 Technical and Dashboard Data

When you access the Drew website or dashboard, or authenticate through Discord OAuth, we may process limited technical and account-related information, including:

- Discord OAuth identifiers and authentication information;
- IP address;
- session identifiers;
- device, browser, and user-agent information;
- request timestamps;
- security and access logs;
- diagnostic and error information.

We use this information to authenticate users, maintain sessions, protect the Services, prevent abuse, diagnose technical issues, and maintain service reliability.

### 3.7 Discord OAuth Authentication

When you sign in to the Drew dashboard using Discord OAuth, Drew receives the Discord account information made available through the authorization process. Drew uses this information to authenticate you and associate your dashboard account with your Discord account.

Depending on the OAuth scopes authorized, this information may include your Discord user ID, username or display name, avatar, and email address. Drew requests only the Discord OAuth scopes required to operate the dashboard and licensing system. If additional scopes are requested in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

### 3.8 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We currently use only strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies required for authentication, security, and session management. We do not currently use advertising cookies.

If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will provide additional information and, where required by applicable law, obtain consent before placing them.

### 3.9 Infrastructure and Service Providers

We use third-party services and infrastructure to operate the Services. Depending on the relevant Service, these may include:

- **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure**, for hosting, infrastructure, and Stane-managed PostgreSQL and Redis deployments used for database, caching, and operational data storage;
- **Cloudflare**, for DNS, security, content delivery, and network protection;
- **Vercel**, for website or dashboard hosting and deployment;
- **Sentry**, for error monitoring and diagnostic reporting;
- **Lemon Squeezy**, for payment, subscription, and purchase administration;
- **Discord**, for authentication, bot functionality, and platform integration.

These providers may process limited personal data only as necessary to provide their services to us or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Retention periods may vary depending on the category of information and the purpose of processing.

- **Error and diagnostic logs:** retained for up to **three (3) months**, then deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.
- **Session and security logs:** retained for up to **ninety (90) days**, then deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.
- **Server configuration and License Slot information:** retained while necessary to provide Drew to the relevant server and, where applicable, for up to **ninety (90) days** after the associated License becomes inactive.
- **Account and License information:** retained while the account or License remains active and for up to **twelve (12) months** afterward where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal compliance.
- **Purchase and billing information:** retained only as necessary for subscription administration and for the periods required by applicable tax, accounting, consumer-protection, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legal obligations.
- **Support and communications:** retained for up to **twelve (12) months** after the last interaction, or longer where necessary for legal or dispute-resolution purposes.

When information is no longer required, we will delete it or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

### Account Deletion
