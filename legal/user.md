You may replace the Discord Server assigned to a License Slot through the Drew dashboard.

Each License Slot is subject to a **seven (7) day cooldown** following a server change.

The cooldown applies independently to each License Slot.

During the cooldown period:

- the Slot may not be reassigned to another Server;
- the Slot remains associated with the previously assigned Server (if still active);
- you may continue to use Drew on the currently assigned Server subject to your Subscription status.

Stane may apply reasonable additional restrictions where necessary to prevent abuse, fraud, or attempts to circumvent License limits.

A License Slot may become inactive if:

Inactive Slots do not count against your active Server limit but remain subject to the Terms of Service regarding reactivation.
